## Apps de salud
- Categoría: **Fitness / actividad física** (no es una app médica).
- **Health Connect** (solo lectura). Texto por cada tipo de dato:
  - **Steps**: Shown on the home screen with the 7-day average, and used to estimate daily activity load in the training plan.
  - **Active calories burned**: Shown on the home screen next to steps, as part of the user's daily activity summary.
  - **Resting heart rate**: Used to detect when the user is not recovered (resting HR above their 30-day baseline) and adapt the next training days; shown in Profile → Health.
  - **Heart rate variability**: Same as resting heart rate: a recovery signal that adapts the training plan; shown in Profile → Health.
  - **Sleep**: Average sleep of the last 3 days is used to adapt the plan when the user sleeps little; shown in Profile → Health and in the Sleep calculator.
- Uso general (en inglés):
  > Zancavolt is a running and strength training app. It reads (never writes) these data from Health Connect only after the user grants permission, to show daily activity and recovery and to adapt the user's training plan. Data is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with third parties. The user can revoke access at any time in Health Connect or in Profile → Health.

## Servicio en primer plano (ubicación)
Permiso `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION`, tipo **location**. La app **no** pide ubicación en segundo plano (`ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`).
- Tarea: **Seguimiento de actividad física / GPS iniciado por el usuario**.
- Descripción (en inglés):
  > When the user taps "Record with GPS" and starts a run, walk or ride, the app starts a foreground service with a persistent notification that shows time, distance and average pace. It records the GPS route while the screen is off so the workout is not lost. The service stops when the user finishes or discards the recording.
- Impacto si se retrasa o se corta (en inglés):
  > The recorded route and distance would have gaps or be lost, and the workout stats (distance, pace, splits) would be wrong.
- **Vídeo** (enlace de YouTube «oculto», 30-60 s), grabado en el móvil con la grabación de pantalla:
  1. Abrir Zancavolt → botón + → «Grabar con GPS».
  2. Aceptar el permiso de ubicación y el de notificaciones.
  3. Pulsar «Correr» y andar unos pasos.
  4. Bloquear la pantalla / bajar la cortina: se ve la notificación «Corriendo · tiempo / km · ritmo».
  5. Volver a la app y pulsar «Terminar».
